How it works

From race goal to daily workout, adjusted as you train.

  1. Tell it about your race

    Race distance, date, current fitness, and how many days a week you can train. Takes a couple of minutes.

  2. Get a plan built for you

    Paceline generates a full training plan tuned to those inputs — not a template picked off a shelf.

  3. It adapts as training happens

    Miss workouts, run behind schedule, or show signs you need recovery (via HRV and resting heart rate), and Paceline trims upcoming long runs automatically — it always tells you why, and never rewrites your plan without asking.

FAQ

Good to know

Is Paceline free to use?

Yes. Generating an adaptive training plan, automatic adjustments, and syncing across devices are all free. Paceline Pro ($6.99/month or $49.99/year, with a 7-day free trial) adds manual plan tuning and long-term progress trends on top.

What platforms does Paceline run on?

Android and iOS.

Do I need a smartwatch or fitness tracker?

No — Paceline works from the workouts you log in the app. Connecting a device through Health Connect or Apple Health is optional, and lets Paceline factor in recovery signals like HRV and resting heart rate for smarter adjustments.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Generating a plan requires a free account and an internet connection. Once it's generated, your plan and workouts stay on your device.

What happens to my data?

Full details are in the privacy policy — including exactly what's collected, what recovery data is used for, and how to delete your account.
