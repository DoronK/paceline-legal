A half marathon is 21.1 km. This sample assumes you already have a running routine: it opens with 2 km and 3 km weekday runs and a 6 km Sunday run. If that Sunday session is a large jump from what you do now, allow more base-building time instead of squeezing the plan into twelve weeks.

How this sample was built

This is an example generated from Paceline's baseline plan engine, reviewed on 18 November 2026. Inputs: beginner level, Tuesday/Thursday/Sunday running, Sunday long run, no core sessions, no target time and no recent-fitness override. It uses the standard 12-week window, with optional generator features off. It is not a personalised plan or a copy of a particular runner's plan.

The Sunday run reaches 16 km before dropping to 9.5 km in week 11. Race week has one short shakeout and the race. The race is longer than the peak training run; this table is an example, not a guarantee of readiness.

Thursday alternates an easy run with a tempo session in the build weeks. The table shows workout titles and distances, not all session instructions or pace guidance. Race week is lighter than a typical three-run week.

What does a three-run week look like?

In a typical week, this sample schedules runs on Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday, with no running planned on the other four days. Sunday is the long run. Race week is lighter, with a short shakeout and the race, rather than three training runs. This is one fixed example, not a promise that three runs a week is right for everyone. Compare the first week with your current routine before choosing it.

What changes in the app?

Paceline builds a plan from your race choice, dates and running schedule. This fixed table cannot react when your circumstances change. You can review your plan in the app and move workouts; for a longer gap, see how missed training weeks are handled. Changes are not a promise that missed sessions can always be made up.

Paceline is a planning app for Android, in English. It does not record runs, send workouts to your watch or provide voice coaching. Building a plan needs an account and an internet connection. For the full scope, see what the free app does and does not do.

Common questions

Does a beginner half-marathon plan mean no running experience?

No. Beginner describes this generator setting, not a promise that the plan starts from zero. Its first Sunday run is 6 km. Check that opening week against your current training before using it.

Is this the plan everyone gets?

No. These are the stated baseline inputs. Your dates, selected days, fitness inputs and app settings can produce a different plan. App behaviour can also change after this page's review date.
