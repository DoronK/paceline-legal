Marathon pace chart: finish time to pace per km and per mile

Checked 27 November 2026. The numbers are arithmetic, calculated from a 42.195 km course. General information, not an individual training prescription.

Divide your goal finish time by 42.195 km to get the pace you would need to hold the whole way. A 4:00:00 marathon is about 5:41 per km, or 9:09 per mile.

What the chart does not tell you

  • It is not a forecast. It shows what a goal time costs per kilometre. It does not say whether you can run it.
  • Race pace is not training pace. Most training runs are slower and easy. Marathon pace is one of several efforts in a plan, not every run.
  • Courses are not flat and days are not perfect. Hills, heat, wind and crowds at the start all move your real splits around the even-pace line.
  • Distance on a watch. Watches often read slightly over 42.195 km because of the path you actually run, so your watch pace can differ a little from the chart.

How to use it

Pick a goal time you have a reason to believe, such as a recent race result, then look at the pace it implies. If that pace feels far beyond a recent hard effort, a slower goal may be the better plan. Paceline has sample marathon plans for different goal times: sub-5, sub-4:30, sub-3:30 and sub-3. For a first marathon see the beginner marathon plan.

Pace is only one part of a plan. How many days you run matters too, see how many days a week to run, and how to ease off before race day in what is a taper.
