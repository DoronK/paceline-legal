Health and safety: not medical advice

Running is physical exercise and carries a risk of injury or, rarely, serious harm. Paceline is a training tool. It is not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

  • Talk to a doctor before you start a new training plan, especially if you are new to running, returning from injury or illness, pregnant, or have a heart, lung, joint or other health condition.
  • Listen to your body. Stop and get medical help if you feel chest pain, faintness, severe shortness of breath or sharp pain. Skip or ease a workout whenever it doesn't feel right; the plan will adapt.
  • Run safely. Watch for traffic, terrain, heat and weather. You decide where, when and how hard you run.
  • Estimates are estimates. Paces, finish-time estimates, suggested settings, weather forecasts and plan adjustments are approximate and can be wrong. They are not a promise of any result.

Some parts of Paceline use AI. For example, the AI-tailored plan option asks an AI model for planning numbers, which our server then checks and turns into your plan. AI output can be wrong or unsuitable for you. Use your own judgment.

Third-party services

Paceline works with services run by other companies. Some are optional and only used if you turn them on:

  • Google: Sign-In, Play Billing, Health Connect and push notifications.
