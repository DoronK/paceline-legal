In short

  • An account is required to use Paceline — your plan is generated on our server, with no offline mode.
  • Health data and location are only ever read if you separately opt into the specific feature that needs them.
  • We don't sell your data, and there's no advertising or third-party analytics SDK in the app. Crash reports go to Sentry so we can fix bugs.
  • Your data lives with our backend provider (Supabase). The few other services we use are listed under Third parties.

What we collect

Account information

Creating an account is required to use Paceline — building your training plan happens on our server and needs you to be signed in, with no offline mode. You can sign up with an email address and password, or with one-tap Google Sign-In. If you sign in with Google, Google processes that sign-in per its own privacy policy; we receive only your email address and Google account ID from it, the same as we'd receive from an email sign-up. Your password (for email sign-up) is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store it in plain text.

Profile and training data

Your running experience level, goals, weight, chosen race and goal time, training schedule preferences, and the training plan we generate for you, including past plans you've replaced. If you complete or reschedule a workout, we record that too.

Health and fitness data — optional

Activity sync. If you choose to connect Apple Health or Health Connect, we read your exercise sessions and the distance, steps and calories burned recorded with them, including past activities, to match them against your planned workouts. For a matched run we keep only its distance, duration, pace and source app, stored with your plan so it syncs across your devices.

Recovery insights. If you separately opt in, we also read your heart-rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate and sleep sessions (time in bed; sleep on Android only). These are used only to decide whether upcoming long runs in your plan should be trimmed back. They're processed and kept on your device only, as a small rolling 14-day summary, and are never uploaded to our servers.

Both are optional and off by default; declining either doesn't affect the rest of the app. Health data is used only to provide these features — never for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone. Our use of data received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Location — optional

If you use the race-week readiness brief (a Paceline Pro feature) and tap its weather-forecast prompt, the app requests your device's approximate location — rounded to about 1.1 km before it ever leaves your device — and sends it to our weather-data provider, Open-Meteo, to fetch a forecast for race day. We don't store this location, use it for any other purpose, or request it anywhere else in the app. If you decline or never tap that prompt, no location data is ever read or sent.

In the same brief you can instead type your race's city to get a heat-adjusted race goal. What you type is sent to Open-Meteo's place search, and the chosen city's coordinates to its forecast service. The chosen city (its name, coordinates and time zone) and your race start time are saved with your plan.

Subscription and purchase status

If you subscribe to Paceline Pro, we verify your purchase with Google Play's Developer API and store whether you have an active subscription. We don't receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Google Play Billing.

Notifications

A reminder for a missed long run is scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. Other reminders (a long run tomorrow, the start of your taper week, a missed workout) are sent by our server through Firebase Cloud Messaging. For these we store your device's push token, platform (Android or iOS), time zone (so reminders arrive at a sensible local hour), your per-reminder on/off settings, and a log of which reminders were sent (so you never get the same one twice). Signing out deletes your push token and settings. You can turn each reminder off in Profile → Notifications, or turn off notifications for Paceline in your device settings.

App usage events

We record a small, fixed set of in-app events against your account — for example which onboarding step you reached, whether plan generation succeeded, workouts completed, and paywall views and purchases — with the app version, platform and a random per-launch session ID. They're stored in our own database, not sent to any analytics company, and used only to understand where the app works and where people get stuck.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report is sent to Sentry. It contains the error and stack trace, your device model and OS version, the app version, and your account ID (a random identifier, not your email) so we can tell whether one person or many are affected. It doesn't include your training plan, health data or location.

Sign-in protection

Sign-up, sign-in and password reset are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that the request comes from a real person. Cloudflare processes technical signals from your device (such as IP address and browser characteristics) for this check, under its own privacy policy.

How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Generate and adjust your training plan.
  • Sync your profile and plan across your devices.
  • Fetch a race-day weather forecast, if you ask for one.
  • Verify and grant Paceline Pro subscription access.
  • Show you reminders you've asked for.
  • Keep sign-in secure and find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • Understand how the app is used, so we can improve it.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent your data to any third party.

Where your data is stored

Your profile and plan data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts our database and authentication. Supabase acts as our data processor and doesn't use your data for its own purposes.

Your choices and rights

  • You can decline or later disconnect Apple Health / Health Connect access, and separately decline or revoke recovery-insights consent, at any time from the app's Profile → Health section.
  • You can decline the race-week weather forecast's location request at any time it's asked; it's never required to use the app.
  • You can turn each push reminder on or off in Profile → Notifications.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see Retention & deletion.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account, profile, training plans, usage events, subscription status and reminder log for as long as your account exists, including past plans you've replaced. Data kept only on your device (recovery summaries, on-device reminders) is removed when you uninstall the app. Crash reports are deleted automatically by Sentry after at most 90 days.

To delete your account, email deltakernellabs@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us that address). We'll delete your account and all associated data from our database — profile, plans, usage events, subscription record, push token and reminder log — within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel that in the Play Store first.

Security

All data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and our database provider encrypts it at rest. Every table is protected by row-level security, so a signed-in user can only ever read or change their own data. Server secrets (such as API keys) are never shipped in the app, and passwords are handled by Supabase's authentication service, never stored by us in plain text.

Children's privacy

Paceline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
